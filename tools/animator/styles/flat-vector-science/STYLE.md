# Flat-Vector Science · v1

The exact helper library, sound kit and drawing/finishing functions are from the
user-supplied reference. `examples/reference-ai.js` is the original complete style
example. `examples/silk-road.js` contains the actual custom illustrations and shot
choreography of the approved Silk Roads experiment. `reference.png` shows its
rendered frames. They demonstrate the medium, not facts or props to transplant.

Use deep navy-to-violet gradients; rounded custom paths with multi-stop gradients;
stepped crescent shadows, pale rims and soft glows; seeded stars/grain/mottling;
bold rounded Nunito and Fredoka text; white/yellow/pink/cyan labels. Dotted arrows
draw on. Easing has a gentle overshoot. Subjects fill the shot and explain one idea.
Typography, clear labels and framing survive phone-sized playback.

The finish runs automatically every frame, unchanged at overlay alpha .12 for
mottling and .09 for animated grain. H and SOUND are included unchanged. Build
new topic-specific drawings with Canvas functions and reuse them in the storyboard
and full animation. Use 1280×720 logical coordinates; delivery is 1920×1080/30fps.

Plan transitions between visual shots independently of narration segments. Reuse
objects and transform compositions for connected ideas; use object wipes or glow
dives for real changes of subject, scale, place or time. Allow quiet holds for
learning. Do not make arbitrary edits every two seconds just to create activity.

Use the original score's short plucks, boops, filtered noise and resolving chords.
Map sounds to the same event constants as visual changes. Effects stay below voice.
Keep actual historical/geographic/scientific relationships accurate, and explicitly
identify schematic representations. The star field expresses the medium.

Quality target: coherent illustration and shading, balanced full-frame composition,
legible measured text, continuous object motion, meaningful transitions, and
purposeful visual development. Reject generic card decks, unexplained doodles,
faceted silhouettes, clipping, and dense captions.

## Worked storyboard direction (adapt the medium to the lesson)

Silk Roads hook: object IDs `silk-ribbon`, `route-network`, `oasis-cities`.
Open on a large curved pink silk ribbon; pull back to reveal the schematic route
network. Close with the same ribbon reduced beside the first city. The title
shrinks into the corner so the explanation can occupy the frame.

Trade beat: carry `route-network` and `oasis-cities` over unchanged in identity.
Introduce `caravan` and `trade-goods`. Follow the caravan along one branching path;
move the goods between merchants at an oasis. Close with the goods in the next
merchant's hands. The arrow is an explanation of exchange, not arbitrary decoration.

Ideas beat: carry cities forward, lower the goods, and introduce a manuscript and
idea motifs along the same connections. Pull back to the completed network and
hold the short takeaway. Audio segments can end while these objects remain on
screen. A genuine time/place change can instead use a foreground object wipe.
For another topic, invent appropriate objects and relationships while preserving
this specificity of opening state, change, closing state, camera and continuity.
